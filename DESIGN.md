# Design System: Veloce Atelier & Private Proving Grounds

## 1. Brand Philosophy & Aesthetic
- **Atmosphere:** High-end hypercar atelier, aerodynamic speed, stealth bespoke luxury, motorsport engineering.
- **Color Identity:** Strict Dual-Palette pairing of Obsidian Black Green (#020F06) with Performance Ruby Red (#FA353F).
- **Typography:** Wide Geometric Racing Display (Syncopate) paired with Aerodynamic Technical Sans (Exo 2) and Telemetry Monospace (JetBrains Mono).
